Format & Scoring

Match Format: Main draw uses best-of-3 tiebreak sets for doubles; consolation format follows posted tournament regulations.
Tiebreaks: The main draw matches and playoff for 3rd place will be the Best of 3 Tiebreak Sets with a 10-Point Match Tiebreak played in lieu of a 3rd set. Up to seminfinals and 3rd place playoff, all matches will use no-ad scoring. First Match Loser Consolation matches will be the Best of 3 Short Sets (4 game sets) with a 7-point Match Tiebreak played in lieu of a 3rd Set.
