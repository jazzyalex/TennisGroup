Format & Scoring

Match Format: Note that in the event of inclement weather the USTA Regulations permit the Referee to shorten the match format from those described below.
Tiebreaks: Main Draw and 3rd Place Playoff Match Format: Best of 3 Tiebreak Sets (6-game sets) with a 10-Point Match Tiebreak played in lieu of the 3rd set. Consolation Draw Match Format: Best of 3 Short Sets (4-game sets) with a 7-Point Match Tiebreak played in lieu of the 3rd set. (A 7-point Set Tiebreak is played at 4 games all in each set.) This format will also be used in the unlikely event a round robin of 5 players is played.
